Privacy for your payout files

Your CSV data stays on your device. Reconciliation happens inside your browser. We do not receive your source files or reports.

Data stored locally

Your current workspace, CSV rows, column mappings, results, and paid snapshots use browser IndexedDB.

Your license token and its latest verification result use localStorage.

Use “Clear workspace” and “Remove stored license” to delete this data. Clearing site data also removes everything.

Demo data

The demo uses sample data in memory. It does not read or write your real workspace.

License verification

When you supply a license, the app sends only that token to the Sociobot billing API.

CSV data and casefile contents are not sent. Sociobot/Dodo processes the information needed for a purchase.

Network and analytics

The app has no ads, behavioral analytics, tracking pixels, remote fonts, or third-party scripts.

The hosting layer may keep standard security logs. The app works offline after its first successful visit.

Exports

Markdown and CSV reports hide references by default. They do not include mapped customer names or emails.

If you turn off reference redaction, you control the exported file.

Contact

Send privacy requests to privacy@sociobot.in.
